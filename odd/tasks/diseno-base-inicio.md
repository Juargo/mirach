# Figma design into the native apps — foundation and Inicio

Objective: bring the Figma design (file Mirach-UI, page Diseño) into `apps/ios` and `apps/android`, starting with the design foundation (text styles from tokens, shared components) and the Inicio screen (catalogue 05, composition approved on frame 19, node `198:3594`).
Branch `feat/diseno-base-inicio`. Delivery strategy: `ask-on-risk` (forecast about 1,500 authored lines, so the chain strategy is asked before any PR). Runners: iOS `xcodebuild … -only-testing:MirachTests test` (Swift Testing), Android `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug lint`, tokens `pnpm design:check` and `node --test scripts/*.test.mjs`.

## Specs

- **S1 — Scope.** The user chose "Base + Inicio (Recommended)": "Primero la base en ambas apps: estilos de texto generados desde los tokens, espaciado y radio por token, y los componentes Aviso, Estado, barras con meta y media dona. Después el Inicio (05) con esa base. Las pantallas nuevas quedan como features aparte." (L3). Out of scope: the tab bar and navigation titles (the tabs stay Resumen, Subir, Categorías, Perfil), every other screen, new screens, API changes, and replacing spacing or radius literals in screens other than the Inicio.
- **S2 — Text styles generated from tokens.** `typography.text-style.*` in `design/tokens.json` (titulo 28/34 w600, numero-grande 40/44 w600, cuerpo 16/24 w400, cuerpo-destacado 16/24 w600, boton 16/20 w600, etiqueta 13/16 w500, mini 11/14 w500, all Inter) is emitted by each generator into the generated token file and exposed as one API per app. iOS: the styles scale with Dynamic Type (`relativeTo:` the nearest system style) and use the bundled Inter faces. Android: `TextStyle`s in `sp` with the Inter family and the token line height. The existing `Font.mirach(_ style: Font.TextStyle)` (iOS) and the Material `MirachTypography` (Android) stay for the screens that are out of scope. New code in this feature uses `MirachSpacing` and `MirachRadius` instead of literals. The generator tests cover the new output, and the generated files are regenerated (CI fails when they are stale).
- **S3 — Shared components**, in each app's `Core/Design` (iOS) or `core/design` (Android), built from tokens only:
  - **Estado**: a 10pt square mark filled with `estado.{bien,cerca,sobre}` plus the word from `MirachCopy.Estado` in etiqueta, colored `estado.bien-text`, `estado.cerca-text` or `estado.sobre` (the design has no separate sobre text token). Gap 6. Never color alone (VIS-02); read by screen readers as the word only.
  - **Aviso** (action strip): background `base.card`, minimum height 44, padding 16 horizontal and 12 vertical, message in etiqueta `base.foreground` that takes the free width and wraps, action label in etiqueta at the trailing edge, gap 12, the whole strip is one button. Variant `bordered`: 1pt inside border in `base.input` (Figma `color/borde/fuerte`). No icon, no red.
  - **Bucket bar**: header row with the bucket name in cuerpo-destacado and the Estado at the trailing edge (omitted when the bucket has no state); a track 10pt high in `base.card` with a fill in the bucket color whose width is the real share of income (0–100%, clamped) and a target tick 2pt wide and 18pt high in `base.foreground`, centered on the target share and vertically centered on the track; caption in etiqueta `base.muted-foreground` with tabular figures, format «56% de 50% · $700.000» (real %, target %, amount spent). Gaps: 4 inside a bar, 12 between bars.
  - **Half donut**: two concentric rings centered on the trailing edge of the screen so only the leading half is visible (visible width 150, height 300). Outer ring: diameter 300, inner radius ratio 0.74, one segment per bucket in its bucket color, sized by its real share of the sum of the three real shares; not drawn when that sum is 0. Inner ring: diameter 208, inner radius ratio 0.80, same colors at 40% opacity, segments sized by target share. Both sweep the visible 180° from the top (Necesidades) through Gustos to the bottom (Ahorro), with a 1.5° gap between segments. Percent labels in 13pt semibold at the middle of each segment: outer labels in `base.background` on Necesidades and Gustos and in `base.foreground` on Ahorro (white on jade is 3.51:1, the documented contrast exception); inner labels in `base.foreground`. The donut is hidden from screen readers because the bars carry the same data.
- **S4 — Inicio (catalogue 05) composition**, top to bottom, with the data `GET /api/resumen` already returns:
  1. Month selector: month name centered in cuerpo-destacado, previous and next chevrons with targets of at least 44pt (48dp on Android). Behavior unchanged.
  2. Month verdict as the headline: a 16pt square mark in the `estadoMes` color and the text in titulo. Copy: bien «Dentro de la meta», cerca «Cerca de la meta», sobre «Sobre la meta» (assumption: only «Sobre la meta» exists in Figma; the other two follow its pattern). Not shown when `estadoMes` is null.
  3. The three bucket bars in a leading column and the half donut at the trailing edge, then the legend «Anillo de afuera: tu mes. Anillo de adentro: tu meta.» in etiqueta `base.muted-foreground`, trailing-aligned. Each bar keeps the navigation to the bucket detail that the current bucket row has. At large text sizes (iOS accessibility Dynamic Type sizes, Android `fontScale ≥ 1.3`) the bars take the full width and the donut and its legend are not shown (assumption).
  4. Income row: «Ingresos del mes» in etiqueta `base.muted-foreground` and the value «$1.250.000 · declarado» or «· detectado» in etiqueta `base.foreground` with tabular figures. It keeps whatever navigation the current income card has on each platform.
  Loading, error, empty month (catalogue 11) and `sinIngreso` states keep their current behavior. The apps do not compute business values (ADR-001): percentages, amounts and states come from the API models already mapped.
- **S5 — The traffic light is shown again.** The user chose "Mostrarlo como en Figma (Recommended)": "El Inicio muestra el veredicto del mes como titular y el estado de cada bloque con su marca y palabra. Revierte la decisión del 5 de octubre y queda registrado en el documento de seguimiento." (L4). Screen-reader text for each bar includes the state word again.
- **S6 — Not built, because the contract has no field (catalogue gap 15, #136) or the flow does not exist in the apps:** «Datos hasta el …», the month phrase («Necesidades: 6 puntos sobre tu meta.»), «Sin gastar», and the three Inicio notices (statement ready, «Te faltan … de ingreso para ordenar tu mes», sin clasificar). The Aviso component is delivered with tests but the Inicio does not render it yet.
- **S7 — Figma first.** Frames 05 (`189:3751`) and 05b (`196:3972`) get the composition approved on frame 19 before the code lands, and `design/README.md` and the catalogue say what changed.
